## 🎨 Naturalness Features

### ✅ Contractions (Heavy Usage)
- **Standard:** I'm, you're, we're, they're, it's, that's
- **Gonna/Wanna/Gotta:** "I'm gonna grab coffee", "You wanna hang out?"
- **Advanced:** shoulda, coulda, woulda, mighta
- **Native-level:** tryna, prolly, dunno, lemme, gimme
- **Very colloquial:** finna, ain't, I'mma, y'all, outta

### ✅ Natural Fillers
- "like", "you know", "I mean", "honestly", "seriously"
- "to be honest", "real quick", "by the way"
- "at the end of the day", "long story short"

### ✅ Run-On Patterns
```
"I mean, they're basically gonna do whatever they want anyway"
"Here's the thing, we really gotta leave right now or we'll be late"
"To tell you the truth, I'm getting kinda worried about the whole thing"
```

### ✅ Natural Sentence Fragments
```
"Look, we gotta move the needle on this thing"
"Look, the ball's in your court now, so..."
"Don't get me wrong here, I absolutely love it, but..."
```

## 📁 File Format

**File:** `data/clips-v2.csv`

**Columns:**
1. `id` - Unique identifier (clip-practice-v2-001 to 300)
2. `transcript` - Full text of what's spoken
3. `difficulty_cefr` - CEFR level (A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2)
4. `focus_areas` - Comma-separated skills (idiom, reduction, phrasal_verb, etc.)
5. `situation` - Context (work, daily, travel, media, formal)
6. `length_sec` - Duration in seconds (3-11 sec)
7. `clip_type` - Always "practice"
8. `content_type` - Category (idiom, phrasal_verb, reduction, conversation, filler)

**Example Row:**
```csv
clip-practice-v2-001,"Let's touch base later today",A2,"idiom,connected_speech",work,4,practice,idiom
```
